# Physical hatch and paper composition (M4)

`hatch3(surface, familiesOrFaceCallback, options)` captures immutable per-face families. Each family has an ID, physical paper spacing, paper angle and optional phase/attributes. A second family provides crosshatch. Bare lengths use the execution's drawable frame; explicit `mm` lengths retain physical paper pitch under zoom. Face callbacks evaluate once during capture, so a modeling attribute can control spacing, direction or retention without rerunning modeling for each camera.

The resolved camera lifts paper rulings to world planes. The shared section kernel intersects each face's fixed triangles, preserving source-face phase across triangulation. Perspective intersections retain barycentric model positions and support triangle IDs; transformed/mirrored instances use the same ownership. Folded polygons use documented piecewise triangle support, not a planar approximation. Near/far clipping determines the available ruling bounds; page overscan is retained for ordinary stroke modifiers. Edge-on faces emit no hatch, outer boundary coincidences are suppressed, and explicit ruling/candidate limits fail rather than silently dropping geometry.

Hatch, section and feature-edge curves use the same visibility classification and ordinary source-chain stroke pipeline. Per-family and per-face attributes remain inspectable. Changing a downstream pen or dash can reuse classification; changing hatch families requires a new snapshot. Trusted immutable surface captures can be shared by sections and hatch without cloning the mesh per face. Captured source mismatches are rejected.
