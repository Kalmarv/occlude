---
title: occlude
description: Plotter-native creative coding. The pen is the medium.
---

Occlude is a library and a studio for drawing with a pen plotter. Shapes can hide what is beneath them, and the engine computes the exact visible strokes. Everything interesting is data you can hold and feed to the next operation.

- **Reference** — one entry per word: the signature, a paragraph, one example.
- **Examples** — one page per drawing, with the whole sketch and what it leans on.

The studio is at [occlude.ivyhq.xyz](/), where every example here can be opened and edited.
